# Photograph map for interior pages

Edit `PAGE_PHOTOS` in `tools/generate_portal.py` to add, swap or remove a photograph, then run `python3 tools/build.py`.
All photographs are existing authentic files in `public/ResidentialSchoolBanikuppa/assets/images/` and are shown whole (never cropped).

| Page | Photographs | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ScienceLabs.html | chemistrylab, physicslab, biologylab | Captioned as the Banikuppa residential campus laboratories (the only laboratory photographs in the project) |
| Activities.html | sportsevent, sportsevent1, houses, audi4 | Sports drill, sports-day winners, house competitions, annual-day programme (Banikuppa campus) |
| Transport.html | classroom, library, computerlab, hostel, dinninghall | Banikuppa campus facilities |

Residential School pages (about, academics, activities, annual, classroom, clubs, computerlab, facilities, gallery, hostel, laboratories, library, masjid, sports, ...) already carry matching photographs and were left unchanged.
Institutions.html and index.html already use photographs and were left unchanged.
