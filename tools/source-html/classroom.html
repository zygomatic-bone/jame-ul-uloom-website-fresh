Residential Primary & High School, Banikuppa

 

Class rooms

Jame-ul-uloom Boys Residential school believes in the following dictum delivering the lessons “I HEAR , I FORGET, I SEE, I RECALL, I DO, I UNDERSTAND.” All teachers endeavor to prepare their lessons and deliver them in such a way that the child becomes a participant in the learning process. The lessons are complimented by practical sessions and access of information on the internet. A total holistic approach is adopted to remove the burden of learning and make the process of learning a joyful experience with out hang-ups and hiccups. The lessons are delivered in the class room by using state of the art audio visual equipment such as overhead projectors and LCD projectors.
