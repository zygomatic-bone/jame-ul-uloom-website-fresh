Build Your Future. Make a Difference.

Jame ul uloom is a community institution that began with two pupils in the basement of the masjid and has grown into a family of schools and colleges. If you would like to help it grow further, we would like to hear from you.

Why join us

Education that shapes character

  • Work with purpose

    The institution’s aim is the upliftment of the community and the building of character, alongside formal education.

  • A place that has grown

    Started in 1983-84 with two pupils in the basement of the masjid, the schools now teach around 3,500 pupils, according to the 2013–14 school diary.

  • Many ways to serve

    From classrooms and workshops to computer labs, the residential campus and the office, there are different ways to contribute.
