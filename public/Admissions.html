Every Great Journey Begins With a First Step.

Learn how admission works at each institution, see what to bring, and send us a simple enquiry. We are glad you are here.

The journey

Five steps from curiosity to classroom

  1. 1

    Choose

    Pick the institution and the class or course. Each one has its own page with what the institution has published.

  2. 2

    Ask

    Send the short enquiry below or call the office. Seats, dates and current fees are confirmed by the office, not on this website.

  3. 3

    Gather

    Prepare the documents on the checklist. Colleges ask for attested or photocopies; originals are shown at admission.

  4. 4

    Apply

    Colleges: fill the prescribed form in your own handwriting and hand it to the office during working hours before the last date. Schools: admission depends on seats in the class.

  5. 5

    Verify

    The office checks the documents. Admissions to the Pre University College are provisional until approved by the P.U.C. board.

Programmes

What each institution publishes

Open a programme to see its published eligibility and requirements. Dates and current fees change from year to year, so please confirm them with the office.

Nursery, primary & high schoolEnglish-medium school for girls and boys, attached to Jamia Masjid.
  • Open to both girls and boys; admission is made on the basis of seats in the particular class.
  • A birth certificate from the Corporation’s Chief Registrar of Births, with the child’s name entered, is required for pre-primary and above.
  • A transfer certificate from the previous school, countersigned by the B.E.O. of the Education Department (children from other states: countersigned by the D.D.P.I. of that state or district).
  • Fees once paid are not refunded on any account.
Read the Admissions Diary 2013–14
Pre University College for GirlsA two-year pre-university course.
  • Open to candidates who have passed the Karnataka S.S.L.C. examination or an examination the board recognises as equivalent.
  • Part 1 languages: English (compulsory) and one of Urdu, Hindi or Kannada. Part 2: Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology; or Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy, Computer Science.
  • The college page lists the fee structure it published. Please confirm the current fees with the office.
See the PU College admissions page
Degree College (B.Com) & BCADegree courses for girls.
  • The prescribed application form is filled in your own handwriting and submitted to the office during working hours before the last date.
  • Attested or photocopies of the documents on the checklist must accompany the form.
  • For fees and eligibility, please ask the college office (BCA office: 0826709915).
See the Degree College page
MMI-NCPUL Computer CentreA computer course of 1000 hours, from July to June.
  • Intake is 60 students a year, in two batches of 30. Each batch studies three hours a day: one and a half hours of theory and one and a half of practicals.
  • Modules cover information technology, business systems and web application development.
See the course and fee details
Industrial Training InstituteNCVT trades of two years.
  • Trades: Fitter, MRAC (Mechanic Refrigeration & Air Conditioning) and MMV (Mechanic Motor Vehicle), each of two years.
  • Eligibility: S.S.L.C. Seating capacity is 42 in each trade.
  • For admission dates and seats, please contact the institute office.
See the ITI admissions page
