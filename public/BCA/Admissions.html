Admissions — Degree College for Girls (B.C.A)

OverviewPrincipalAdmissionsStaffFacilitiesRulesContactCareersAll Institutions

For BCA combinations, fees and eligibility, please contact the college office (0826709915).

Combinations offered

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com).

Fee structure

Despite being an unaided institution the fee structure in comparison to other colleges is very nominal. The fee should be paid within the stipulated date; fee once paid will not be refunded.

Admission procedure

  1. The qualified candidates seeking admission are required to fill up the prescribed application form in their own hand writing and submit the same to the office during working hours within the last date prescribed.
  2. The applications should be accompanied by attested or Xerox copies of the following:
    1. Statement of marks obtained in the qualifying examination.
    2. Transfer certificate from the institution last attended.
    3. Latest caste certificate in case of the students belonging to SC/ST/BC/BT.
    4. Migration certificate if applicable.
    5. Six passport size and two stamp size photos.
    6. Original certificates must be submitted at the time of admission for proper verification and other formalities with the P.U Board.
    7. All admissions are provisional until they are approved by the P.U.C board.
    8. Producing fake documents or furnishing wrong information will attract criminal prosecution.
