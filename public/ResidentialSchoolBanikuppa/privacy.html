Privacy

Privacy & photo use

How this website uses photographs of pupils and staff, and how to ask for one to be reviewed or removed.

Why photographs appear on this website

This website uses photographs of the campus, classrooms, hostel, activities and the people of Jame Ul Uloom Residential School, Banikuppa, to show visitors what school life is like. Some photographs show pupils, teachers, staff, trustees and members of the governing body.

Pupils

Photographs on this site do not carry pupils’ names. We ask that visitors do not copy, edit, redistribute or use photographs of pupils for any purpose other than viewing them on this website.

Asking for a photograph to be reviewed or removed

If you appear in a photograph, or you are the parent or guardian of a pupil who does, and you would like it reviewed, replaced or removed, please contact the school using the details below. Please tell us which page the photograph is on. We will review the request promptly and remove the photograph from this website where requested.

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts, comments, analytics or advertising. It does not ask you for personal information, and it does not set tracking cookies. The only item saved in your browser is your light/dark display preference, which stays on your own device. If you contact the school by telephone or email, the school will use the details you give only to respond to you.

Search engines and sharing

We ask search engines not to list the photographs in this website in their image search results. We cannot control copies that other people make or share.
