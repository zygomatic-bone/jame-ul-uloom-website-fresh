The institution

Education with purpose.

Endeavor: Jame Masjid & Muslim Charitable Fund Trust, a non-profit organization, is well known all over the country for its services in the field of education. Over the years it has endeavored to create and establish educational institutions to cater to the needs of different sections of the society and has been successful in establishing a host of institutions starting from nursery school to degree colleges where hundreds and thousands of students belonging to the privileged and under privileged sections of societies have realized their dreams and aspirations depending on their need, ability and affordability. Jame-ul-uloom Residential School is yet another path-breaking venture of the Jame Masjid and Muslim Charitable Fund Trust to create world-class excellence and facilities in the field of education and cater to the needs of such parents who are on the lookout for such excellence and facilities to educate their children. Jame-ul-uloom Residential School is offering “quality education and sports facilities at throwaway price”. Avail this opportunity and build the future of your child. The school fee is highly affordable and not beyond the means of a quality.

The Ambiance: It is far away from maddening crowd, pollution and din of the city 26 kilometer from Bangalore city, on the Bangalore–Mysore four-lane highway reachable in just 35 to 40 minutes from city market. A very congenial and conducive environment which makes learning a joyful experience. The campus hosts a school building, a beautiful Mosque, a dining hall which can accommodate 500 students at a time and an auditorium. It has dormitories for students and residential quarters for the resident staff as well. The campus is well secured and under the surveillance of security guards.
