Our Vision

In our school we take pride in the following:

  1. To cherish the quality of simplicity of a person’s behaviour, relationship and lifestyle
  2. To be always friendly, polite and courteous with everyone everywhere, and cleanliness of the school premises
  3. To initiate and promote experimentation and innovativeness in education.
  4. To inculcate the habit of punctuality, regularity and discipline.

The main objectives of Jame-ul-uloom are:

  1. To pursue excellence and to mould the character
  2. To develop the spirit of national integration and create a sense of Indianness among children.
  3. To initiate and promote experimentation and innovativeness in education.
  4. To inculcate the habit of punctuality, regularity and discipline.

Rules and Admission

  1. Jame-ul-uloom school is open to both girls and boys.
  2. Admission will be made on the basis of seats in particular class.
  3. Birth Certificate from the Corporation Chief Registrar of Birth, with the child’s name entered should be produced without fail in respect of children seeking admission to the pre-primary standard and above. A transfer certificate from educational institution previously attended by the child should be duly countersigned by the B.E.O of Education Department.
  4. Children who have studied in schools in states other than Karnataka should have their transfer certificate duly countersigned by D.D.P.I of the state or District concerned.
  5. Fees once paid will not be refunded on any account.

High-Lights of Jame-Ul-Uloom

This prestigious School of ours is a linguistic minority institution. This is a recognised institution which was started in the year 1983-84, with just two pupils in the basement of the Musjid. By the grace of Allah, and with the full co-operation of the office bearers and Trustees, the institutions have expanded by leaps and bounds. At present we have around 3,500 pupils, studying in different courses.

This institution of Jame-ul-uloom which is attached to Jamia Masjid, imparts Deeniyath and moral education to all its pupils apart from the formal education. This is to mould the character of the young and to develop in them a desire to live an honourable life in the modern society.

For Primary Classes

  1. Promotion from class I to IV Std. shall be decided on the basis of continuous comprehensive assessment made every month of the work done in the class and assignments given to the children, in Semester Examination.
  2. Home work is given to encourage and develop independent study skill.
  3. Home work time table is given to all students who are required to complete the work as assigned by subject teachers.
  4. Class assessments are considered to evaluate their overall performance throughout the year. Remedial classes for slow learners are conducted on a regular basis to help and boost their academic performance.

Jame-ul-uloom High School

Started in 1990-91 this school has so far sent 20 batches of students for S.S.L.C Examination of them 11 batches gave us 100% results. During the year 2012-2013, 158 students appeared for the SSLC Examination. Out of 158 students 24 students obtained distinctions and the percentage pass was 100 %.

Extra Curricular Activities

All round development of the personality of a child is the goal of education and besides academics students are given opportunities in extra curricular activities like Quiz, debate, fancy dress, painting, creative writing etc. to develop their own talents. Students attend competitions organised by other schools and DHIE.

Teachers day and children’s day, national festivals like Independence day and Republic day are some of the occasions when the children exhibit their talents in various fields.

Compulsory Computer Education

It is an accepted fact that in the modern age, life will be blank without computers. With a view to keep our students abreast with the modern information, computer education is made compulsory right from IV Standard. Arrangement for computer education is made separately for girls and boys in different blocks.

Examination & Promotion

A time table is given a week before a test/Examination. Pupils absent for any reason are not re-examined. In case of an unforeseen holiday, the test/examination of the day will be conducted after the last paper as per the time table.

The final Examination report for all students from 1 Std to IX Std will be issued in April 2014 to confirm the result of the year’s work.

75 % attendance is mandatory for all the students.

Promotion is decided by the head mistress in consultation with teachers. Answer papers of final Examination shall not be given to parents/guardians. Answer papers of other tests/examinations will be shown to parents/guardians.

School Discipline

  1. Our School is known for its values. A man may achieve any height he aspires, but if he is ignorant of the basic values like discipline, punctuality, good mannerism, respect, love, compassion etc., he has not gained much in life.
  2. The School follows certain norms to help the students achieve these goals. All students are expected to be well aware of all the rules and also to follow them without any lapse.
  3. As the medium of instruction is English, children should speak in English in the school premises with their teachers and companions to develop their conversation ability. Parents should encourage their children to talk in English, in addition with their mother-tongue at home with brothers and sisters.
  4. Students should bring the school diary everyday to school. They should bring to the class the necessary text books and note books as per the time table.
  5. It is necessary that the students cultivate the best etiquette and conduct themselves well in school and wherever they go. It is important that the students bear in mind the fact that the school is judged by their conduct, behaviour and performance. They should always greet their teachers, elders and friends in an amiable and audible tone of voice whenever and wherever they meet them in school or outside.
  6. Cellphones are strictly prohibited.
  7. A school canteen is provided in the premises. Students are prohibited to buy eatables from outside the school campus. No student will be sent home in between school hours. In case of emergency the parents should contact the Headmistress.
  8. The school authorities reserve the right to dismiss those students whose progress in studies is unsatisfactory.
  9. Students are responsible for all personal belongings.
  10. Students are expected to be within the school campus by 8.40 a.m. Repeated late-coming and disinterest in studies will not be tolerated. Discipline in the assembly, in the classrooms and in between the periods should be maintained.
  11. Leave of absence will not be granted without prior submission of leave application from the parent or guardian. In case of absence without a leave letter, on returning to school the students must bring the leave note signed by the parent or guardian.
  12. Neatness and cleanliness are insisted; students are expected to come to school daily in freshly laundered uniform with clean socks and well polished shoes. Any student who comes to school in a slovenly condition will be excluded from class or sent home.
  13. Attendance is mandatory on the first and last day of each term.

Results

Pupils who fail in VIII and IX standard will not be permitted to continue their studies in our institution. There is no provision for reconsideration of failures. Result once declared according to the rules of the education department, cannot be altered. Parents are requested not to take the trouble of meeting the Head Mistress or Management for this purpose.

Educational Excursion

Pupils are taken to visit places of historical or geographical interest. All students are expected to participate in these excursions, whenever such excursions are arranged separately for both boys and girls.

While on the Bus / Van

The students who are using school transport are expected strictly to adhere to the following rules.

  1. Be on the allotted time at the point of boarding.
  2. Always be seated till your destination.
  3. Do not pick up fight or tease the fellow passengers.
  4. Do not cause inconvenience to others with your belongings in the way.
  5. Do not damage the bus or van in any way.
  6. Respect all the rules set by the driver, helper, teacher in charge.
  7. Maintain perfect discipline with your best behavior.

Dr. Hans Raj Bharadwaj, Hon’ble Governor, Government of Karnataka, was the Chief Guest. Alijanab R. Roshan Baig Saheb, M.L.A. Jayamahal, Bangalore and Alijanab Abdul Qadeer Sahab, Secretary, Allama Iqbal Education Society and Shaheen Group of Institutions, Bidar were the Guests of Honour.

The Awards and Prizes to meritorious students of Jame-ul-uloom College Bachelor of Computer Application, Composite P.U. College, English High School, Bangalore and Residential High School, Bannikuppe, Industrial Training Institute. Special feature of the function was that awards were given to the City Muslim Organizations. Trustees of Jame-ul-uloom, many dignitaries of the City, parents and other invitees attended the function.

Special prizes are given to the students class wise every year for regular attendance, cleanliness, best performance in studies throughout the year. Literary activities such as debate, quiz, essay writing, sports and athletic and team games are taught and competitions were conducted and prizes are awarded to the winners.

Newspaper in Education

Deccan Herald in Education programme was introduced in our school since 2004-05. It is a programme designed to help the students identify and harness it as a classroom tool. The newspaper is a vibrant current medium that can be effectively used by the students to relate the textual facts to reality, thus bringing enthusiasm into classroom situations, besides increasing the child’s general awareness it also gives the child a basic reading habit and enhancing his/her knowledge of the changing world hand in hand with the text book.

The DHIE hold workshops on various topics. These workshops are conducted by highly talented and qualified personalities of Deccan Herald. Children participate in these workshops enthusiastically. The child is therefore exposed to a wider range of knowledge, besides the regular curriculum.
