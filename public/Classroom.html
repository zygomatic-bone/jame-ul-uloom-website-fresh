Smart Classrooms

Computerised classroom teaching at Jame-ul-uloom School.

An opportunity has been created by Jame-ul-uloom School for the students who can access a unique way of computerised education in their curriculum. Hoping that the students shall enhance their knowledge and make learning more interactive and interesting.

Digital classes play a crucial role in the life of a student as well as teachers and cover the subjects like English, Mathematics, Science, Social Sciences, Comprehension passages, nursery rhymes with animations, videos and examination guidelines etc. Thus the school has worked a massive project to develop an information which will cater to the needs of the students in all subjects.

Classroom photographs of individual institutions are on their own Facilities pages: PU College · Degree College · BCA · Boys High School · Girls High School.
