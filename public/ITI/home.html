Welcome to Jame-ul-Uloom Industrial Training Institute (ITI)

OverviewMessageAdmissionsStaffManagementWorkshopsLibrary & Computer LabSportsContactAll Institutions

JAMIA MASJID & MUSLIM CHARITABLE FUND TRUST (JM&MCF) is one of the reputed trusts, established in the year 1939 with a mission to support the nation by educating people and to create a good society. The trust, with a core mission to establish a world class university, has started schools and colleges in and around Bangalore. One among the campuses is in Shikshanagar; the Board of Trustees were keen to develop the technically skilled manpower, which can fetch better employment and provide an opportunity for a trained technician to start his own industrial unit after completion of Technical Training courses.

The Board of Trustees took the decision to start I.T.I courses in the trades of Fitter and Mechanic Refrigeration & Air Conditioning from 2nd September 1996. There was a good response both from trainees and industry for trained manpower. This encouraged the Trust to start one more I.T.I course, in the trade of Mechanic Motor Vehicle, from the year 2002. Soon after, within a short span of time, the good response and the hard work of management and staff made the institute grow faster; now the institute has full sanctioned strength and the campus has spread over 2 acres and 12 guntas of land. The institute is situated in a greenery campus with serene atmosphere under the guidance of an excellent team of faculty members headed by a qualified and experienced Principal. The institute has excellent training infrastructure including labs and workshops with state-of-the-art trainer kits, machineries, seminar and lecture halls and a well stocked library.
